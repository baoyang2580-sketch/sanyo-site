Message from the President

Valuing finite resources, for the future.

We sincerely thank you for your continued support and patronage.

Based in Minamiechizen, Fukui, SANYO CO., LTD. purchases and recycles metal scrap — including copper, brass and aluminium — as well as precious metals and electronic circuit boards. We also focus on pelletizing plastics and purchasing used cars and used machinery. Our mission is to put finite resources back to use in society once again.

What we value most is approaching every transaction with integrity and building trust with our customers. With legal compliance and safety management as our foundation, we are committed to fair appraisals and attentive service, aiming to be a company you can rely on with confidence.

We also cherish our ties with the local community, and we will continue to grow together with it while caring for the surrounding environment and protecting the safety of our team.

Through continuous effort and improvement, we will keep finding new value in resources and contribute to building a sustainable, circular society.

We look forward to your continued support.

SANYO CO., LTD.
Bao Yang, Representative Director
